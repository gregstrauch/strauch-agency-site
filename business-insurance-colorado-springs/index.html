What's different here

Three things about Colorado Springs your policy has to know.

Workers' comp is not optional

Colorado requires workers' compensation for any business with one or more employees, including part-time. Fines run per day. It's the first thing we check on a commercial quote.

Contractors and hail season

Roofers, GCs and remodelers here live on hail work. Commercial auto for the trucks, tools coverage for what's on them, and liability limits that satisfy the GCs and property managers you sub for.

Government and defense work

Subcontracting for the installations, Space Command or a prime means certificates of insurance with specific limits and additional-insured wording. We've seen the requirements and we'll match the policy to them.

Questions we get

Straight answers.

Does my homeowners policy cover my home-based business?

Barely. Most homeowners policies cap business property at a few thousand dollars and exclude business liability entirely. A small in-home business endorsement or a BOP fixes it for very little.

What is a BOP?

A business owner's policy bundles general liability and commercial property into one policy at a lower combined price. It fits most retail, office, restaurant and service businesses under a certain size.

Do I need commercial auto if I use my personal truck for work?

If it's used regularly for business — hauling tools, visiting job sites, deliveries — yes. Personal auto policies exclude business use, and the claim gets denied after the crash, not before.

How fast can I get a certificate of insurance?

Same business day for existing clients, usually within the hour. GCs and landlords don't wait, so neither do we.
