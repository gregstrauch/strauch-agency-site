The real problem

Nobody reads their policy. Then something happens.

Insurance gets sold in ninety seconds by whoever was cheapest that week. It doesn't get explained. So people carry state-minimum limits, a deductible they can't cover, or no umbrella at all — and never know until the day it counts.

You think you're covered

Minimum liability limits in Colorado won't come close to a serious accident. The gap comes out of your savings, your equity, or your paycheck.

Nobody explained it

Deductible, replacement cost, UM/UIM, umbrella. Real terms with real consequences, handed over as a PDF nobody walked you through.

You're a policy number

When you call, you get a queue and a stranger. When something goes wrong at 7pm on a Tuesday, that's exactly the wrong time to meet your agency.
