Home Inventory Checklist

If you had 15 minutes to leave, could you prove what you owned?

Download the PDF

On Instagram or Facebook? Tap ··· at the top right and choose “Open in browser” first, then download.

  1. Video every room, closet, garage, and outbuilding.
  2. Open drawers and closets on camera. Say brand names out loud.
  3. Photograph serial numbers on electronics and appliances.
  4. Save receipts for big purchases.
  5. Store everything in the cloud, not only on your phone.
  6. Update it once a year.

Then check two lines on your policy

  1. Loss of use. If you're out of your home, keep every receipt: hotel, food, mileage. Loss-of-use coverage may reimburse extra living expenses.
  2. Extended replacement cost. Does your policy pay above your dwelling limit if rebuilding runs over?

When you want to check your loss-of-use and replacement cost coverage, we'll go through it with you.
