What's different here

Three things about Colorado Springs your policy has to know.

SGLI ends when service does

Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance is $500,000 and it stops at separation or retirement. VGLI gets expensive fast. A term policy bought while you're healthy and still serving locks in the rate for 20–30 years.

Group coverage isn't portable

Employer life insurance at Memorial, UCHealth, USAA or a defense contractor usually ends the day you leave. One or two times salary rarely covers a Colorado Springs mortgage anyway.

Mortgage math

Median home prices here crossed $450,000. A 30-year term policy that covers the mortgage balance is usually the cheapest life insurance you'll ever buy, and the most useful.

Our checklist

What we look at on every life quote.

  • Need calculated from debts, income replacement and years of dependency
  • Term length matched to the mortgage and the youngest child
  • Whether whole or universal life fits a permanent need (estate, special-needs child, business)
  • Existing SGLI, VGLI, FEGLI or employer coverage counted honestly
  • Beneficiaries set up correctly — contingent beneficiaries too
  • Final-expense coverage for parents when that's the real question

Questions we get

Straight answers.

How much life insurance do I need?

Add the mortgage, other debts, and 5–10 years of income replacement, then subtract what you already have. For most Colorado Springs families with a mortgage and kids it lands between $500,000 and $1.5 million — which in term coverage is far cheaper than people expect.

Term or whole life?

Term covers a period — the years people depend on you — at the lowest cost. Whole life is permanent and builds cash value at a higher premium. Most families need term first; some need both. We'll tell you which and why.

Is SGLI enough?

While you're serving, it's a strong base. It ends at separation, and VGLI premiums climb with age. Buying term now, while healthy and in your 30s or 40s, is the cheapest way to keep coverage after the uniform comes off.

Do I have to take a medical exam?

Depends on the amount and carrier. Many policies under certain limits are issued with a health questionnaire only. We'll tell you before you apply.
