90 days out

  1. Pull your dec pages: auto, renters or homeowners, and life.
  2. Keeping your house here and renting it out? A homeowners policy usually needs to change to a landlord policy before a tenant moves in.

60 days out

  1. Belongings in transit. Check whether your renters or homeowners policy covers your things while they're moving and in storage. The mover's liability is limited.
  2. Your car. Shipping it or driving it cross-country? Confirm it's covered in transit.

30 days out

  1. No gap, no overlap. Set coverage to start at the new place and end at the old one on the right dates.
  2. New state rules. Check registration and insurance requirements for your new state.
  3. Going overseas? U.S. auto policies generally don't cover driving abroad.

On arrival

  1. Update your garaging address.
  2. Walk the new place with your phone camera for a home inventory.

Separating instead of moving: SGLI to VGLI

  1. SGLI continues for 120 days after separation.
  2. Apply for VGLI within 240 days of separation and you don't have to answer health questions.
  3. From day 241 up to 1 year and 120 days, you can still apply, but you'll need proof of good health.
  4. Compare VGLI with private coverage before day 240, so the deadline doesn't make the choice for you.

Source: VA, Apply for VGLI
