What's different here

Three things about Colorado Springs your policy has to know.

The wind/hail deductible

Most Front Range policies now carry a separate wind and hail deductible — often 1–2% of your dwelling coverage, not a flat $1,000. On a $500,000 house that's $5,000–$10,000 out of pocket. You should know that number before the storm.

Wildfire and the west side

Homes in Mountain Shadows, Broadmoor Bluffs, Cedar Heights and up Ute Pass sit in wildfire zones. Some carriers won't write them; others require mitigation. We know which and we'll tell you up front.

Rebuild cost, not market value

Construction costs in El Paso County rose faster than home values. Dwelling coverage should be what it costs to rebuild your house today, with today's labor. We run a replacement-cost estimate on every quote.

Our checklist

What we look at on every home quote.

  • Dwelling limit from a replacement-cost estimate, not the purchase price
  • Wind/hail deductible you understand and can pay
  • Liability limits that match your auto policy
  • Water backup and sump coverage — the most common uncovered claim
  • Scheduled coverage for rings, guns, instruments and collectibles
  • Roof age and material, because it moves both premium and coverage

Questions we get

Straight answers.

Why is my Colorado home insurance so expensive?

Hail. Colorado is consistently among the top three states for hail claims, and carriers price roofs accordingly. Impact-resistant shingles, a newer roof and a higher wind/hail deductible are the levers that move it.

Does homeowners insurance cover wildfire?

Fire, including wildfire, is covered by standard homeowners policies. The question is whether a carrier will write your address at all — that depends on the wildfire zone, defensible space and access.

What does an HOA master policy cover versus my condo policy?

The master policy covers the building; your HO-6 covers your interior, belongings, liability and the master-policy deductible you'd be assessed. That assessment is the piece condo owners most often leave uncovered.

Do I need flood insurance in Colorado Springs?

Homeowners policies exclude flood. If you're near Fountain Creek, Monument Creek or below a burn scar, ask — the 2013 flooding after the Waldo Canyon fire hit homes nobody thought were at risk.
