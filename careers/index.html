Pay & benefits

Producer: $33,600/year base ($2,800/month) plus uncapped commission paid at 3–8% of new-business premium, rising with your monthly volume. Expected total earnings $45,000–$80,000/year depending on production; top tier has no cap.

Client Service Representative: salary plus performance bonus, set by experience and licensing at time of offer.

Benefits (all roles): we pay for your insurance licensing and all training, and all employees receive paid time off.

Posted under the Colorado Equal Pay for Equal Work Act. Ranges are good-faith estimates as of September 2026; actual earnings depend on production and are not a guarantee of income.

Room to grow
