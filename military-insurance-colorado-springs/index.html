What's different here

Three things about Colorado Springs your policy has to know.

PCS to Colorado Springs

Orders in hand, house or apartment not yet found, cars arriving on a carrier. Renters and auto need to be in place the day you sign, and Colorado's UM/UIM and MedPay rules are different from where you came from. One call, both policies, same day.

Deployment and storage

A car sitting for nine months doesn't need full coverage, but it can't go uninsured either — Colorado registration requires proof. We'll set it to comprehensive-only for the deployment and switch it back the week you're home.

Base housing doesn't cover your stuff

Privatized housing at Carson, Peterson and Schriever insures the building. Your household goods, your liability and your hotel if the unit floods are yours to cover. Renters insurance handles all three for less than a tank of gas.

Questions we get

Straight answers.

Do you offer a military discount?

Farmers offers military-related discounts on eligible policies, and we apply them automatically for active duty, Guard, Reserve, retirees and veterans. Ask — we'd rather over-check than miss one.

I'm PCSing here — when should I set up insurance?

Call before you arrive. We'll bind auto and renters to start the day you need them, so you're not scrambling at the housing office or the DMV.

Can I keep my out-of-state policy?

Once you register a vehicle in Colorado you'll need a Colorado policy. Active duty stationed here can often keep home-state registration; we'll tell you which is cheaper for your situation.

Is Greg actually still serving?

Yes — Air Force Reserve, drilling here in Colorado Springs. Drill weekends are covered by the rest of the team; the agency doesn't close. Greg's service is his own; nothing here implies endorsement by the Department of Defense or the Air Force.
